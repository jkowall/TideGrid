## Risk governance

Each risk has a named product/engineering owner before build. Critical residual risks require an executive acceptance record and tested runbook. Risk evidence is reviewed at architecture gates, pilot readiness, GA, and quarterly operations review.

The risk register is not static. Incidents, near misses, support trends, provider changes, penetration tests, restore drills, and new jurisdictions create or re-score risks.
