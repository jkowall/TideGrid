# TideGrid V2 company formation and operating readiness

**Status:** Execution plan; bare-bones formation precedes outreach, but no filing or spend is authorized by this document

**Date:** September 4, 2026

**Jurisdiction:** Florida operating base; United States first

TideGrid remains exploratory. This document is planning guidance, not legal, tax, accounting, insurance, or trademark advice. It does not authorize a filing, purchase, account opening, professional engagement, or public launch.

## Recommendation and timing

Form TideGrid as a single-member, member-managed Florida LLC owned 100% by its sole founder. No customer, adviser, contractor, vendor, or pilot operator receives equity or management authority unless the sole member later approves a documented change. Use that Florida LLC for the paid pilot unless a credible institutional-financing, preferred-stock, or employee-equity requirement exists before formation. A Delaware entity would still need to register and report in Florida, so it adds cost and administration without a current financing benefit.

Form the bare-bones entity before the next operator-validation outreach, after all of the following are true:

- the customer-facing validation brief and internal interview guide are approved and agree with the V2 scope and pricing;
- the legal name, product name, intended domains, and obvious conflicting marks have current preliminary screens;
- the sole owner has decided the public principal and mailing addresses, registered agent, correspondence email, effective date, initial contribution, and any authorized-representative listing in a private filing packet;
- current and former employment and invention-assignment obligations have been reviewed far enough to avoid an unsupported ownership claim;
- the owner accepts the $125 required state filing, public-record exposure, annual-report obligation, and the possible registered-agent and local-registration costs; and
- the required Pompano Beach and Broward registration path has been confirmed or a written timing decision is recorded.

Full legal, tax, insurance, contract, payment, messaging, continuity, production-account, and build-budget readiness is not required merely to file. The bare-bones company may conduct validation but may not sign customer contracts, accept pilot payments, store production customer data, open production provider accounts, or submit a production native app. After the interview and conditional-demand gates pass, complete the separate paid-pilot readiness gate below. The production-build gate still requires three executed recurring agreements and three settled $2,250 setup payments.

## Florida LLC execution checklist

### Before filing

1. Search the proposed legal name in [Sunbiz](https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/search), similar marks in the [USPTO trademark database](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search), and the intended domains through an accredited registrar or [ICANN Lookup](https://lookup.icann.org/). A state name filing does not establish trademark clearance.
2. Confirm and record one member, a 100% membership interest, member-managed status, the initial cash and pre-formation-property contribution, treatment of pre-formation expenses, reserved sole-member authority, and the intended federal tax classification. Record that there are no outstanding equity interests, options, profit interests, or customer ownership promises. Obtain targeted legal or tax advice before filing only when the employment, intellectual-property, ownership, or tax facts are not straightforward; reserve the full professional package for paid-pilot readiness.
3. Select a Florida registered agent and a legitimate principal and mailing address. A principal may serve as agent, but the agent needs a physical Florida street address and the filing becomes public. A commercial agent is preferable if privacy or reliable availability matters. A registered-agent address is not automatically a general business mailing address.
4. Decide whether the legal entity will be `TideGrid LLC`. A separate Florida fictitious-name registration is needed only if the company trades under a different name.

### Form and organize

1. File Articles of Organization directly through the [Florida Division of Corporations](https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/start-business/efile/fl-llc/).
2. Execute a single-member operating agreement and initial written action of the sole member. Unless counsel deliberately selects a manager-managed structure, do not identify the member as a Florida `MGR`; confirm with the bank whether the public filing needs an authorized representative.
3. Execute a separate signed assignment of pre-formation TideGrid intellectual property from the founder to the LLC. Attach a schedule identifying repositories and commit hashes, domains, product names and marks, designs, documentation, research, and other contributed assets. Exclude third-party and open-source property while recording applicable licenses. Review current and former employment obligations and record whether pre-formation expenses are contributed or reimbursed. Require confidentiality and invention-assignment terms from every later employee and contractor.
4. Maintain a definitive one-line membership ledger showing the sole member's 100% interest and a written sole-member approval log.
5. Apply directly for a free [IRS employer identification number](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number) after the state accepts the entity. Do not pay an EIN filing service.
6. Record the current federal beneficial-ownership conclusion. As of this document's date, [FinCEN states that US-created companies are exempt from BOI reporting](https://www.fincen.gov/boi). Recheck the official page on the filing date because this rule has changed repeatedly. Banks may still request beneficial-owner details under customer due-diligence rules.

### Confirm federal tax treatment

A domestic single-member LLC is generally disregarded for federal income-tax purposes unless it elects corporate treatment. Use that default initially unless the CPA recommends and the sole member approves a different classification. Do not elect S-corporation treatment automatically at formation or assume tax savings before credible revenue, net profit, payroll cost, and reasonable owner compensation are modeled.

For federal income-tax reporting, a disregarded single-member LLC generally supplies the owner's name and taxpayer identification number on Form W-9. The LLC can still use its own EIN for banking, employment taxes, and provider requirements. Confirm the completed form and all elections with the CPA. See the [IRS single-member LLC guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/single-member-limited-liability-companies).

### Establish operations

1. Open one company checking account and company card using the EIN, filed Articles, operating agreement, and requested ownership information. Keep company and personal money separate.
2. Configure bookkeeping before the first expense or invoice. Preserve receipts and distinguish software subscriptions, managed-booking fees, setup services, pass-through reimbursements, customer-specific delivery cost, and shared product development.
3. Start a simple monthly close and bank-reconciliation routine. Prepare the W-9, accounts-receivable, sales-tax, estimated-tax, and annual-filing calendar before the first invoice or tax deadline, with narrow CPA confirmation when needed.
4. Use the [Florida Department of Revenue registration wizard](https://floridarevenue.com/taxes/eservices/Pages/registration.aspx) to determine which state tax accounts are required. Complete a multistate SaaS nexus review before invoicing customers outside Florida, not merely to conduct validation interviews.
5. If the operating address is in Pompano Beach, ask the city to confirm the zoning and [Business Tax Receipt](https://www.pompanobeachfl.gov/government/code-compliance/business-tax-receipt-division/business-tax-receipt-division-faqs) path in writing, then obtain the city receipt before the [Broward County Business Tax Receipt](https://www.browardtax.org/business-tax-receipt/) when required. Home-based businesses are not automatically exempt, so do not assume outreach or sales activity is exempt.
6. Put the corporate domain, registrar, DNS, source control, email, password manager, and research materials under company-controlled identities with hardware-backed MFA and tested recovery. Move or create production cloud, Stripe platform, monitoring, messaging, and other live-customer accounts only after the paid-pilot-readiness gate authorizes them. Keep the founder as the individually verified beneficial owner, control person, and primary administrator where a provider requires one. Never share the owner's login or place credentials in this repository.
7. Record the sole-member authority matrix. Only the sole member has standing authority to sign customer contracts, borrow, change bank or Stripe payout details, grant production administration, issue ownership, or approve material spending. High-risk actions still require written sole-member approval and audit records; the plan must not claim a second-person approval that does not exist.

## Delaware comparison

| Path | Formation and Florida qualification | Recurring state and agent floor | Fit |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Florida LLC | $125 | $138.75 Florida annual report, plus any commercial agent | Recommended for the bootstrapped paid pilot |
| Delaware LLC operating from Florida | $110 Delaware formation plus $125 Florida foreign-LLC registration, plus a Delaware agent | $300 Delaware annual tax plus $138.75 Florida annual report and agent cost | More administration without a current benefit |
| Delaware C corporation operating from Florida | $109 minimum Delaware formation plus $70 required Florida foreign-corporation registration and a Delaware agent | $50 Delaware report, at least $175 or typically $400 franchise tax depending on the calculation method, $150 Florida report, and agent cost | Reconsider for institutional capital, preferred stock, or an equity plan |

The [Delaware Division of Corporations](https://corp.delaware.gov/howtoform/) requires a Delaware registered agent. Delaware LLCs owe $300 by June 1 and do not file an annual report. Delaware corporations file by March 1 and pay a $50 report fee plus franchise tax. The [official franchise-tax calculator guidance](https://corp.delaware.gov/frtaxcalc/) states a $175 minimum under the Authorized Shares method and a $400 minimum under the Assumed Par Value Capital method.

With a $100 registered agent, a Delaware C corporation registered in Florida has a base recurring state-compliance cost of roughly $475 at the $175 tax minimum or $700 at the $400 tax minimum, before local receipts, tax preparation, or income tax. Do not choose a Delaware LLC as an interim compromise. If the financing facts change, have counsel and the CPA compare a fresh Delaware C corporation with conversion of the Florida LLC, including tax and IP-transfer consequences.

## Paid-pilot legal, insurance, and accounting package

### Company and ownership

- filed Articles, single-member operating agreement, initial written action, definitive one-line membership ledger, EIN letter, and correctly classified W-9;
- signed founder-to-LLC assignment and schedule for all pre-formation TideGrid IP, domains, content, designs, documentation, research, and repositories, with third-party property and licenses identified;
- confidentiality, work-for-hire where legally effective, and invention-assignment terms for employees, contractors, and advisers;
- written sole-member authority for contracts, borrowing, refunds, bank and Stripe payout changes, production administration, ownership issuance, and material spending; and
- an explicit statement that a pilot operator receives no equity, governance, board, profit-share, intellectual-property, exclusivity, partnership, or agency right.

### Customer and guest terms

- master services agreement, order form, Pilot Native offer addendum, Managed Launch statement of work, and pass-through-cost schedule;
- precise definition of net managed booking value, audit evidence, invoicing, annual minimum, reversals, refunds, taxes, and termination data export;
- operator-as-merchant-of-record language aligned with Stripe direct charges, including disputes, chargebacks, reserves, statement descriptors, Stripe-set processing fees, and Stripe negative-balance loss responsibility;
- data processing agreement, privacy policy, guest terms, security schedule, subprocessor list, retention rules, incident duties, support terms, and liability allocation;
- operator responsibility for approved waiver text, policies, tax settings, business records, messaging consent, brand rights, and lawful customer communications;
- TideGrid responsibility for rendering the approved immutable waiver version and preserving evidence, without representing that TideGrid drafted or certified the waiver;
- native-app account ownership, delegated access, content approvals, review delays, rejection and resubmission limits, app transfer, suspension, and termination duties; and
- SMS sender registration, transactional consent, STOP/START/HELP, replies, carrier charges, and the exclusion of marketing campaigns.

### Insurance and finance controls

- technology errors and omissions, cyber and privacy, and commercial general liability coverage in force before live use;
- broker confirmation that payment interfaces, waiver evidence, SMS, native apps, data incidents, and contractual indemnities are not excluded from the chosen coverage;
- workers' compensation, employment coverage, and directors and officers coverage reviewed when hiring or outside financing begins;
- one company bank account and one TideGrid billing account for TideGrid revenue and expenses;
- no receipt, custody, or commingling of operator booking funds; and
- monthly reconciliation of subscriptions, managed-booking fees, setup revenue, credits, pass-through charges, processor settlements, and the pilot-customer subsidy ledger.

Stripe direct charges should place guest payments on each operator's connected account. The operator remains merchant of record and is responsible to guests for refunds and disputes. For the pilot, Stripe must set and collect processing fees and accept connected-account negative-balance loss through `defaults.responsibilities.fees_collector = stripe` and `defaults.responsibilities.losses_collector = stripe`, or a written Managed Risk equivalent. TideGrid invoices its own subscription and managed-booking fee separately. Require Stripe confirmation before signing the pilot contracts because merchant-of-record status alone does not assign unrecoverable loss. See [Stripe's SaaS platform guidance](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/saas-platforms-and-marketplaces), [connected-account configuration](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/accounts-v2/connected-account-configuration), [Managed Risk](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/risk-management/managed-risk), and [direct-charge documentation](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/direct-charges).

## Sole-owner continuity

Single ownership removes cofounder coordination but creates key-person risk. Before live bookings, align the operating agreement with the founder's will or trust, durable power of attorney, and digital-asset instructions so a successor member can be admitted after death and an authorized representative can act during incapacity. Florida permits documented delegation and representative action in defined circumstances, while an LLC with no member for 90 consecutive days can face dissolution. Counsel must tailor the documents; see [Florida Statutes 605.04071](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/Statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0600-0699/0605/Sections/0605.04071.html), [605.0504](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/STATUTES/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0600-0699/0605/Sections/0605.0504.html), and [605.0701](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/Statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&URL=0600-0699/0605/Sections/0605.0701.html).

- Name a non-equity emergency custodian or contracted administrator with narrow activation conditions, documented revocation, and no ordinary commercial authority or standing production access.
- Maintain an encrypted break-glass packet outside the founder's personal inbox and primary laptop. Include provider ownership and transfer instructions, account inventory, recovery-code locations, current runbooks, renewal dates, backup payment methods, and customer contacts without duplicating live secrets into this repository.
- Define customer notification, data export, billing suspension, refund handling, safe service shutdown, and native-app unpublishing or transfer procedures for extended unavailability.
- Have someone other than the primary operator rehearse account recovery, retrieve the current runbook inventory, and observe a technical restore before the first live booking and at least annually thereafter.
- Promise only the business-hours support the owner can provide. Contracted backup is required before TideGrid offers after-hours human coverage, 24-hour support, or uptime credits.

The custodian, attorney, CPA, contractor, and automated agents remain service providers. None becomes a founder, member, officer, or independent TideGrid decision-maker through this continuity role.

## Bare-bones formation exit gate

Bare-bones formation is complete only when evidence exists for every item below:

- Sunbiz shows the entity active and the registered agent has accepted appointment;
- the single-member operating agreement, initial written action, 100% ownership record, sole-member authority matrix, and IP assignment schedule are executed after the employment and ownership screen;
- the EIN confirmation, bank account, bookkeeping ledger, and compliance calendar exist;
- required Pompano Beach and Broward County registrations, written not-required conclusions, or written timing decisions are recorded;
- the company-controlled domain, email, source, and research storage use hardware-backed MFA and tested recovery;
- FinCEN BOI status was rechecked on the formation date and the result was recorded; and
- the sole owner can produce the private formation file, current costs, renewals, and account owners without relying on an undocumented account.

After this gate, TideGrid may conduct the planned validation outreach. Formation does not authorize a customer contract, deposit, production account, live customer data, production native submission, or production build.

## Paid-pilot readiness exit gate

Paid-pilot readiness is complete only after the interview and conditional-demand gates pass and evidence exists for every item below:

- required Florida tax registrations and the correctly classified W-9 are complete before invoicing;
- counsel has approved the pilot agreement set and the operator-as-merchant-of-record presentation;
- required insurance is bound with reviewed exclusions and limits;
- company-controlled production cloud, Stripe, messaging, monitoring, and security-recovery accounts are operational with two hardware keys and tested recovery;
- the succession, incapacity, emergency-custodian, customer-export, safe-shutdown, and independent recovery-drill evidence is complete;
- an approved bottom-up build and delivery budget, contingency, funding source, runway limit, and customer refund or long-stop treatment are recorded;
- the public company, privacy, terms, support, security-contact, and status pages are ready;
- each prospective pilot operator's entity, account, store, domain, content, and Stripe prerequisites are verified; and
- the sole owner or properly activated emergency custodian can produce the current costs, contracts, provider owners, customer-export procedure, and safe-shutdown procedure without relying on a personal inbox or undocumented account.

Only after the paid-pilot-readiness gate may TideGrid execute the pilot agreements and accept the three setup payments. Only after all three agreements are signed and all three payments settle may the full production build begin.
